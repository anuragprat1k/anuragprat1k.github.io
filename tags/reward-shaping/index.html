Reward Shaping

Discovery Needs Dense Rewards?

Fine-tuning a language model with RLVR is known to sharpen it — the policy concentrates on known solutions, boosting pass@1 while pass@k collapses (Yue et al., 2025; Zhao et al., 2025). This is increasingly treated as a fundamental property of RL on language models. But it shouldn’t be.
