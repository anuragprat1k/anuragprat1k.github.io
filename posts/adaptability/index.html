Notes on Adaptability

April 5, 2026

Framing#

Adaptability is learning from experience, and some might even say the true measure of intelligence. However, all the major breakthroughs of AI in the past decade (AlphaGo, ChatGPT, Claude Code) have been at training time, encoding a notion of adaptability in their weights that doesn’t change as the model interacts with the world.

LLM RL for Coding I’ll spend the first 10 minutes covering my most recent work on adapting LLMs for coding, which will touch upon how this notion exists in llms today. And then I’ll spend the last 10 minutes thinking ahead a bit.

What should adaptable systems look like?

  • Persistent memory - as the AI gets to know you more, it keeps track with your preferences.
  • Skill Learning - it learns to do new things

What does adaption mean practically though? There is a flood of hype words out there - people building simulation environments, automating back office work, AI scientists.

What did AI look like 5 years ago, circa 2021? ChatGPT wasn’t a thing, we were still writing our own code and painfully debugging it. Models were small, the era of scaling hadn’t arrived yet. 2022 - ChatGPT came and LLMs became a thing. Scaling laws and RLHF. Reasoning models unlocked RLVR. RLVR and scaling unlocked Claude Code and modern era of open LLMs.

What will AI look like in 5 years?

  • continual learning?
  • better reasoning models - better credit assignment for RL
