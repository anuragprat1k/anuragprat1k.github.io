Research Vision

May 7, 2025

A log of research directions I am curious about on the path to making useful agents and corresponding next steps.

Grounding#

All SotA LLM agents today suffer from a lack of grounding. This might be an artifact of many things - autoregressive generation (which is bound to snowball errors as the generation length grows), the post-training recipe of these models which are primed towards being helpful (maybe at the expense of being correct?), generally nascent tool calling ability.

Next step: Build a lightweight advantage verifier that scores actions proposed by LLMs within simulated agentic environments (à la SayCan (Ahn et al., 2022))

Process Rewards#

A lot of recent work utilizes outcome based rewards for RL. Process rewards are hard to define and annotate while outcome based rewards are easier to verify. But in trying to avoid process rewards, are we throwing the baby out with the bathwater? Hypothetically, process rewards should help RL more than outcome rewards.

Next step: Build a PRM prototype using LLMaaJ for a math, then agentic, usecase

Long Horizon Planning#

How does Deep Research work? What are the blockers to scaling it to run for days and months? Can we quantify the natural tendency of an LLM for long horizon tasks - how many steps does it like to go typically?

Next step: Write a simple dumb benchmark that is easy for humans to do forever and see how LLMs compare.

Open-Endedness#

Most post-training regimes are reward-seeking which makes models exploitative by design. But agents need to explore - try unknown actions, ask speculative questions and seek novelty and not just immediate reward. How do we design curiosity objectives that scale to language action spaces and don’t devolve into noise?

Knowledge Discovery#
